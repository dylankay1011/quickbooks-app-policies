Disconnecting

Kay Supply BisTrack Sync · Kay Supply, Inc.

The connection between this application and QuickBooks Online can be revoked at any time from within QuickBooks Online itself. No request to us is required, and revocation takes effect immediately.

How to disconnect

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online as an administrator of the company
  2. Open Settings (the gear icon), then Apps
  3. Find Kay Supply BisTrack Sync in the connected apps list
  4. Choose Disconnect and confirm

What happens then

  • The application's access token is revoked immediately and it can no longer read or write anything in QuickBooks Online
  • Accounting records already posted remain in QuickBooks, unchanged — disconnecting does not delete or reverse any transaction
  • The application stops posting; source exports continue to accumulate but are not recorded until it is reconnected
  • Stored credentials on our side become invalid and are removed
Disconnecting is safe and reversible. See Connecting or reconnecting to restore it.
