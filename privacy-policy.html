Privacy Policy

Kay Supply BisTrack Sync · Last updated 27 August 2026

Kay Supply BisTrack Sync ("the Application") is a private, internal accounting integration operated by Kay Supply, Inc. ("we", "us"). It is not distributed, sold, or made available to the public. It is used solely by our own staff to post our own accounting data into our own QuickBooks Online company file.

Who uses the Application

Access is limited to authorised employees and contractors of Kay Supply, Inc.. There are no external users, no customer accounts, and no sign-up process.

What data the Application accesses

The Application reads daily transaction exports produced by our BisTrack ERP system and, through the Intuit QuickBooks Online API, reads and writes the following in our own company file:

  • Chart of accounts, classes and vendor records — read only, to match transactions correctly
  • Journal entries, bills and vendor credits — read, to detect duplicates before posting
  • Journal entries, bills and vendor credits — written, to record the day's transactions
  • Company information — read once, to confirm the correct company file is connected

This is our own business accounting data. The Application does not access, collect, or process personal information about consumers, customers, or any individual outside our organisation. It does not handle payment card data, bank credentials, payroll, or government identifiers.

How data is stored

Source exports, generated import files and run logs are stored on company-controlled systems and our organisation's Microsoft OneDrive tenant. API credentials are stored locally on the operating machine, outside any shared or synced folder, restricted to the operating user account.

How data is shared

It is not. The Application transmits data only between our BisTrack export files and our own QuickBooks Online company. No data is sent to any third party, no analytics or telemetry is collected, and nothing is sold, rented, or disclosed for advertising or any other purpose.

Retention

Accounting records are retained in QuickBooks Online under our normal record-keeping policy. Export files, generated import files and run logs are retained as accounting workpapers. Credentials are retained only while the integration is in use and are revoked when it is retired.

Security

Access to QuickBooks Online uses Intuit's OAuth 2.0 authorisation. We do not store QuickBooks usernames or passwords at any point. Refresh tokens are stored in a file restricted to the operating user account and are rotated automatically on each use. Access can be revoked at any time from within QuickBooks Online.
