Kay Supply BisTrack Sync

Internal accounting integration · Kay Supply, Inc.

This is a private, internal application. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up. It is used only by authorised staff of Kay Supply, Inc., against Kay Supply's own QuickBooks Online company file.

What it does

Each business day, our BisTrack ERP system produces three transaction exports — general ledger journals, accounts payable vouchers, and vendor credits. This application validates those exports and records them in QuickBooks Online as journal entries, bills and vendor credits.

Every batch is checked before it is posted: journals must balance, vendors and accounts must already exist in QuickBooks, and any transaction already recorded is detected and skipped. A member of our finance staff reviews and approves each batch before anything is written, and the result is verified afterwards against the source files.

Data handling

  • Processes only Kay Supply's own business accounting data
  • No consumer or customer personal information is accessed
  • No data is shared with, sold to, or transmitted to any third party
  • Access uses Intuit OAuth 2.0; QuickBooks credentials are never stored
