Connecting or reconnecting

Kay Supply BisTrack Sync · Kay Supply, Inc.

There is no self-service connection. This is a private internal integration with a single authorised connection to Kay Supply's own QuickBooks Online company. It is not available to other organisations, and there is no public sign-up or install flow.

Who can connect it

Only an authorised administrator of Kay Supply, Inc. with admin rights on our QuickBooks Online company can establish or re-establish the connection. It is set up once and runs unattended thereafter.

When reconnection is needed

  1. The authorisation was revoked from within QuickBooks Online
  2. The connection went unused long enough for its authorisation to lapse
  3. The integration was intentionally disconnected and is being restored

How to reconnect

Reconnection is performed by our internal administrator, who re-authorises the application against the Kay Supply company file through Intuit's standard OAuth 2.0 authorisation flow and restores the resulting credentials on the machine that runs the integration. No action is required from any other party.
